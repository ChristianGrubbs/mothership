# Getting started

## Before you begin
Use the account provided by your administrator, or choose **Sign in with Google** when the sign-in page offers it. The first Google sign-in shows **Waiting for access** with a request number: send that number to your administrator, who lets you in, then sign in again. Available folders and tools depend on your role and folder grants.

## Steps
1. Sign in and open Home.
2. Open the Library to browse the folders you can access.
3. Use your account menu to review your profile and sign out when finished.

## Expected result
Home shows your account's saved layout. Library access reflects your assigned permissions.

## Troubleshooting
If a folder or tool is missing, ask an administrator to check your role and folder access. Signing in does not grant access to every asset.
