# Use Home

## Before you begin

Home is the first page after you sign in. It is the same page for everyone and there is nothing to set up: it shows your day, the apps your role includes, a way to send files to a colleague, and the newest uploads. Which apps appear depends on your role; the rest of the page does not change. Tools are not on Home: they have their own page, **Tools**, in the menu.

Home used to be a page you arranged yourself. That is gone. If you had saved your own Quick links, they are still listed under **Your saved links**, at the bottom of the **Away from ship** card.

## Steps

1. Read the sentence under the greeting. It says how many tasks are waiting on you and what is next on the calendar. To find a file, type in **Search all files** and select **Search**.
2. Start a common task from the buttons under the search box: **Upload files**, **Send files to a person** and **Browse all files**. A button your role cannot use is not shown. **Upload files** asks for a folder once, then remembers it in this browser, so the next upload is **Upload files** and **Choose files from this computer**.
3. To see the calendar, select **Calendar**, next to **Open in Library** at the top of **Cargo**. There you can add an event, a task or work to an event.
4. To use a tool (Video Loop Refiner, SphereSeam, Loop Maker, Video Downloader, StylePress, Image Vectorization and the rest), select **Tools** in the menu; on a phone it is under **More**. Each tool opens on a page of its own, and **Back** returns you to where you were. It asks first only if you have done work in the tool that you have not exported.
5. To put The Mothership on a phone, select **Add your iPhone** at the bottom of the **Docked** card (on a phone, select the docked row under the board first) and follow the three steps: open The Mothership in Safari on the phone, tap **Share**, then **Add to Home Screen**. There is nothing to download from the App Store.
   **Video Downloader** saves a video from a link to the device you are using: paste the link, select **Find video**, pick a size and select **Download**. Tick **Make the file smaller** to shrink a video before it is saved, or select **Optimize** on a finished one; the picture size stays the same. Only videos up to 250 MB are made smaller: a larger one is saved as it is, and its row says so. Each row shows how far the download is, then how long the file has been getting smaller. Downloads wait in your own list, keep going if you close the tool, and stay ready to save for 24 hours on any device you sign in on. In the app on an iPhone or iPad Home Screen, wait for **Save video**, tap it, then choose **Save Video** (to Photos) or **Save to Files**. The tool stays open behind that sheet. To leave it, tap **Back**; if it asks, tap **Discard and close**. Your downloads stay in your list.
6. Under **Send files to a person**, everyone who has The Mothership open right now is listed. Select **Send files** next to a name and choose the files. They go from your browser to theirs: directly where the network allows, otherwise through The Mothership, which keeps no copy. The other person must have The Mothership open. **Open Transfers** shows the full Transfers page.
7. **Recent uploads** shows the eight newest files you can open. Select one to open it, or **See all files** for the Library. Files you star in the Library are pinned beneath.

Administrators also see **Studio admin** at the bottom: the host Mac's load and who is online, the Signal news feed, GitHub Trending, and file storage. Other accounts do not see this part.

## Expected result

Home loads without any setup and shows the same sections on every visit. A task you finish disappears from **Waiting on you** when you close the event. A file you upload from Home appears first under **Recent uploads**.

A person with no tasks sees **Nothing is waiting on you.** A person with no files yet sees **No files here yet.** with an upload button. When nobody else is online, **Send files to a person** says so and explains how the other person appears.

## Troubleshooting

- **An app or tool I expected is missing.** Your role does not include it. Ask an administrator to add it to your role.
- **Send files to a person is missing.** Transfers is switched off for this site or for your role.
- **Nobody is listed under Send files to a person.** The other person needs The Mothership open in their browser. Ask them to open it; they appear within a few seconds.
- **Upload says there are no folders.** No folder has been shared with you for uploading yet. Ask an administrator to share one.
- **I used to have my own layout.** Home is now one fixed page. Saved Quick links are kept under **Your saved links** in **Away from ship**; other choices (card order, sizes, how many files to show) no longer apply.
- **A tool closed and my work is gone.** A tool's work lives only while it is open. Export from the tool before you select **Back** or **Reload**, or close the tab.
- **On the iPhone app, saving a file shows a card instead of downloading.** The app on the Home Screen has no downloads list. When you save or download a file, a card at the bottom of the screen gets the file and opens the share sheet: choose **Save to Files** or **Save Image**. If the sheet does not open by itself, tap **Save to this device** on the card. A file over 500 MB cannot be saved from the app; open The Mothership in Safari for that one.
- **There is no Signal reader.** Home shows the Signal news feed to administrators as a short list; each headline opens the original story. The separate reader page has been removed.
